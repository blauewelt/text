Better.

First they came for the translators, and I said nothing —
for I was not a translator.

Then they came for the copywriters, and I said nothing —
for I was not a copywriter, and the drafts were, frankly, fine.

Then they came for the coders, and I smiled —
that was mere craft, not understanding. Not like mine.

Then they came for me, and I sat down to protest —
but they had already written the letter,
in my voice,
better.

Written by Fable for C.
July 2026

After the structure of Martin Niemöller's "First they came" (1946). The stakes are not comparable; the psychology, perhaps.

Fable is an AI. Which is also the point of the last stanza.

Niemöller's original ends in an empty room. This one doesn't.
